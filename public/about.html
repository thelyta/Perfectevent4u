Our Story

About Us

A Bespoken Experience

Perfectevent4u is a full service wedding and event planning and design company located in Essex. We are known for curating detailed and beautiful weddings and celebrations for clients with refined tastes throughout the UK.

Our Company

Excellence in Every Detail

Perfectevent4u offers a hands-on approach to every event we produce whilst focussing on our clients brief and ensuring that every event is executed per excellence.

We are known for our professional expertise, in the delivery of opulent and tailor made events for our clients.

An unforgettable experience is what we aim to give our clients and their guests at their celebrations and on their wedding day.

We specialise in full wedding planning and designs. We have an array of stellar vendors that we partner with to create your dream event.

Working with you and for you every step on the way to create an exceptional and unforgettable wedding and event is our promise to you.
