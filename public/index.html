Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 12-18 months in advance for full-service planning. For day-of coordination, 3-6 months is typically sufficient.

Do you travel outside of Essex?

Absolutely! We serve clients throughout London, Essex, and across the UK. Destination weddings can also be arranged.

What is your pricing structure?

Our packages are tailored to each client's needs. We offer full-service planning, partial planning, and day-of coordination. Contact us for a bespoke quote.

Can you work with our existing vendors?

Yes, we are happy to collaborate with vendors you have already selected. We also have a curated list of trusted partners we can recommend.
